# Layout and behaviour

The look, tokens and design decisions are in `docs/brand-system.md`. This file covers how things are built.

## Breakpoints

| Width | What changes |
|---|---|
| `< 810px` | The main one. Header becomes logo and menu button, `--header-h` drops to 60px, the mobile menu opens as a full panel, the home hero's top row and lede hide, and Instinct's scene switches to its phone tier. |
| `< 1000px` | About's card rows, the Experience list and the project detail layout go to one column. The detail rail goes back to normal flow. |
| `< 1100px` | Film grid and UX steps drop to two columns. |

Shared layout: `.page` is capped at `--page-max` (1320px) with `--gutter` sides, and `.section` uses `--section` spacing. Sizes step down fluidly with `clamp()`.

## Shell

- **Header:** wordmark, Work, About, Contact, DOSEN button, theme toggle. Work also lights up on `/web`, `/motion` and `/ux`, and About on `/stack`. On project pages the wordmark plays a small splash for the page's discipline (`disciplineForPath`). It stays still on discipline pages, where the title builds instead.
- **Mobile menu:** locks scroll, closes on Escape and after navigating, returns focus. It holds the nav, discipline pills, DOSEN, the toggle and socials.
- **End card:** the black footer on every page. The name draws with the pen when it scrolls into view. It also holds the availability line, email, link columns and Back to top.
- **Backdrop:** `BrandBackdrop` draws the dot grid and floating tools behind every page. `NeonCursor` follows the pointer.

## Scroll and entrances

- Lenis smooth scroll (`lerp: 0.1`), not mounted under reduced motion. Scroll restoration is manual and the swap resets scroll.
- `Reveal`: sections below the fold rise 28px and fade in once. Server markup is always visible, and anything already on screen at hydration is left alone.
- Home intro: `HeroLockup` under `html[data-intro]`, with the hero's other content fading in once the pen finishes.

## Discipline titles

`DisciplineTitle.jsx` keeps the h1 as real text and lays a temporary SVG over it.

- **Measuring:** `measure()` takes each letter's position from the real text with `Range`, and the baseline from a throwaway inline-block, so kerning, letter-spacing and wrapping always match. Each letter is then drawn from `src/brand/titleGlyphs.js`. A title that can't be measured, or whose fonts aren't in within 400ms, just shows.
- **Outlines:** generated by `scripts/title-outlines.py` from Outfit at weight 600 (`.page-title`). Run it again if a title gains a letter it doesn't have yet.
- **States:** `data-state` goes `waiting`, `playing`, `landed` (1.6s of gleam and flare on the real text), `done`, and the real text is transparent until `landed`. On a first load, `html[data-intro]` hides it before paint, with a CSS fallback at 3s in case the script never runs.
- **Timing:** the drawing is measured and mounted at once, held on its first frame, under the cut. It starts through `afterUncover`, as the pulse clears the title, on the build clock in `src/transitions/titleClock.js`: a slow start that ramps to 1.25x over 650ms, so the build carries on from the pulse rather than waiting for it to end or popping in. A `requestAnimationFrame` loop in `DisciplineTitle` sets every animation's `currentTime` from that clock (the UX build reads the same `clock` ref). A build lasts until its last animation ends, converted to real time by `realDuration()`. The UX intro is hidden until the reflow ends, because the two-line title reaches over it.
- **Motion's plan:** `penPlan()` sets each letter's rhythm, stroke speed, nodes and handles from a seeded `wobble()`, so it looks hand-drawn but is the same on every visit. Handles use the curve's own tangents from `titleGlyphs.js`, so each lies along the outline.
- **Landing:** the glass hero gets `.is-struck` for 1.6s, one strike of the edge glow plus a surge of its pool of blue (`tb-surge`).
- **Web beam:** a WAAPI animation timed to the letter locks (`lock()` in `CodeBuild`). **UX:** a `requestAnimationFrame` loop drives the frame, letters and trailing outlines; its grid and guides use the card's measured box.
- **Card glow:** `.discipline-hero-copy::after` holds a mild pool of `--neon-rgb`, shaped per discipline in `--hero-glow` and kept to the right of the text. It drifts with the cursor and comes up from 45% as the title lands.
- **Light:** the filter is `#title-neon` through the `--neon-title` token, dark theme only. Light keeps flat blue lines.
- **Reduced motion:** no overlay at all.

To inspect a frame from the console, stop the loop by patching `requestAnimationFrame` to a no-op once the overlay is up, then set `currentTime` on `document.getAnimations()` under `.title-build-fx`. Patch `setTimeout` first, or the overlay goes when the build finishes.

## Page transitions

`App.jsx` swaps the page under cover, and `SiteShell` gets the displayed pathname so the header, backdrop and end card change with it. `document.title` is set from `metaFor` at the swap.

A capture-phase click listener (`src/transitions/origin.js`) notes where each click happened and picks one of two cuts:

- **Selection box** (`frame.js`, 0.7s hide, 1s show): clicks on `.project-card` or `.gf-sheet`. The blue frame, filled with the card's picture, grows to the screen edges, then shrinks onto the new page's first target in `frame.js` `TARGETS` that is on screen. It re-measures each frame so it follows the rising title.
- **Dot wave** (`dots.js`): everything else, including the back button. A 15px grid with every second dot on the backdrop's 30px grid and its 0.22 parallax. Black dots swell on the leading edge, and `destination-out` holes open onto the live new page on the trailing edge.
  - With the View Transitions API (`snapshot.js`), it is one wave of about 0.72s (`drawWave`, `WAVE`). The old page is the browser's frozen picture, and the new one is clipped to one circle scrubbed from the store's clock, staying inside the solid middle of the band. `BAND_GLOW` lights the band's black, painted `source-atop` so it stays on the black. The overlay and neon cursor have their own `view-transition-name`s so they stay live on top.
  - Elsewhere it covers the screen first (`drawDots`, `PULSE`, about 0.48s then 0.56s).

The store's `uncover` event and `covering` flag gate entrances: `afterUncover` in `arrival.js` starts `Reveal` and `HeroLockup` when the cut has opened over them, and `SiteShell` keys the header splash on `useUncovered()`. Reduced motion and same-page navigation swap immediately.

`prefetch.js` warms a route's top images on link hover, focus or press (`mediaFor`). No route lists a video for now. `warmVideo` stays for when the reveal stage returns.

## Glass

- **`GlassCard`:** `<GlassCard as={Link} to="/x" look="web" className="...">`. The layers sit at `z-index: -1` inside an isolated stacking context, so children need no wrapper. Rules for using it:
  - It has no padding: keep at least 22px, which the contrast figures assume.
  - Consumer classes must not set `background` or `border`, and should not override `position` or `overflow`.
  - For a sticky card, wrap it (see `.detail-rail`).
  - Link cards lift and glow on their own, so don't add your own hover transform.
  - Cards with the same look show identical art, so mix looks along a row.
  - `.stack-card--no-frost` turns off the backdrop blur for pages with many cards.
- **Tokens:** the card sets `--ink`, `--body`, `--soft`, `--accent-strong` and `--on-accent` locally, so child styles work on any look. Don't hardcode text colours inside one.
- **Art:** painted once per recipe at a fixed 480x320 and cached, through one shared queue that spends a few milliseconds a frame. Folders paint before cards. The cursor writes `--gf-px` and `--gf-py`.
- **Light:** `useGlassLight(ref, selector)` writes `--gl-*` on the elements that hold the layers, from one shared loop that runs only while light is moving. Each piece of glass has a `.glass-light` layer above its tint, and a rim light (`.gc-rim-light`, or `.gf-rim-light` on a folder's path). Keep new glass on `GlassCard` and it comes with this.
- **`GlassFolder`:** rest, hover (the panel leans and files lift) and open (files fan out, each a link). Click or Enter opens; Escape or a press outside closes. The key is one arrow link to the discipline page (`action="link"`), and Cmd-click opens it in a new tab.

## Instinct Groove page

- The page is only a hero: Back to Work, the green Visit site button (the only link to the site), the wordmark, a short intro, then the wall extended by `.ig-hero-more` holding one line.
- The live scene runs on every screen size, with WebGL2 and without reduced motion or Save-Data. Otherwise, or if a phone has under 3GB of reported memory, it shows the baked `hero-room` still with the wordmark lettered in CSS.
- Phones (under 810px) get `quality: "phone"` in `hero.js`: `QUALITY.phone` caps the canvas at 1.25 megapixels and shrinks the buffers, MSAA and god-ray taps. Half-size `*-phone.webp` textures bring the download from 6.8MB to about 0.7MB, and the frame rate is capped at 60 through the intro and 30 after. Touch doesn't move the camera. `LAYOUTS.phone` in `vines.js` keeps the ivy around the edges so the sign and copy stay clear.
- A phone falls back to the still if the scene hasn't started in 9s, the GPU context is lost, or frames stay over 110ms at the lowest resolution. `watchFrameRate` first drops resolution by steps to 55% whenever a 30-frame average passes 42ms.
- The camera pulls back with height (`frameHeight`) so the wall keeps its scale. The canvas is capped at 5.5 megapixels on larger screens (`QUALITY.full`) to hold the frame rate.
- After changing the scene, re-bake the plates: with `npm run dev` running, run `npm run bake:instinct` and open each URL it prints in the browser pane. The folder sheet is drawn half again as tall, with the sign as far down, and cropped back (`crop`): drawn at its own size, the sign sat so high that the mural was squeezed into a dark band along the top, with the vignette darkening the bottom.

## Other components

- `ArrowLink`: internal or external, with an underline and two arrows that swap on hover.
- `ButtonLink`: filled blue for the one main action on a screen, outlined for the rest.
- `ProjectCard`: 16:10 media, image scales to 1.025 on hover, selection handles snap on, arrow turns into a filled blue circle.
- YouTube blocks: poster first, then a `youtube-nocookie.com` iframe on click, with no autoplay.
- `RevealStage`: not rendered on any page for now. Its CSS (`.stage*` in `components.css`), the videos and the `video` and `reveal` fields in `disciplines.js` stay so it can come back.

## Frequency Shift laser room

The Frequency Shift site's laser room (`~/Documents/FREQUEMCY 2`, `components/laser-room/`, read only) is ported into `src/scenes/laser-room/` as TypeScript, which Vite compiles and ESLint skips. The files are copied as they are, so a newer version can be copied over. The only differences are options in `scene.ts`, all off unless asked for. `neon: false` leaves the channel letters out and keeps their light. `follow` re-hangs that unseen sign every frame so it sits behind the page's wordmark (`hang` and `followPage`, split out of `placeBay`). `headInBand` hangs the laser head clear of the kicker, and `rigCentre` hangs the rig over the middle of the room. `caseStudy.js` stands in for the site's `mount.ts`, and `FrequencyShiftRoom.jsx` mounts it.

- The stage is fixed behind the whole article, so every section is transparent over it. The sign is built from `mobile-fs-wordmark.svg` (the same artwork as the site's stacked neon wordmark) and placed exactly on the page's `h1`, so the wall's halo, the haze and the god rays come from behind the page's own wordmark. The sign stays there however the camera moves: each frame it is re-hung where the camera sees the wordmark (its offset position minus `scrollY`). Without that, the halo, the gap it cuts in the smoke and its floor reflection drift off the wordmark once the camera turns, and show as a ghost of the letters. The laser rig doesn't follow, and hangs over the middle of the room. The scroll drives the site's camera move: it tilts from the wall to the floor over the first screen, then drifts back to the end of the page. The veil over the room deepens with `--room-travel`.
- The photographs, the browser and the panels stand on the floor as lightboxes (`CARDS` in `caseStudy.js`). The room's light comes back as `--room-sign`, `--room-lasers`, `--room-pink` and `--room-travel` on the article. Panels pulse on the beat (`fs-beat-pulse`, kept in time with the show by `beatClock`).
- Glass comes from the site's "Glass, the buttons' way", and the cursor light is its `glass-light.js` (`src/components/fsGlassLight.js`, mice and trackpads only). Both live in `src/styles/fs-room.css`.
- `data-room` on the article reads `pending` (prerender), `loading`, `live`, or `still`. `still` covers reduced motion, Save-Data, no WebGL2, a phone with under 3GB of memory, a phone that can't start in 9s, and a lost GPU context: it keeps the crowd photograph and its outlined lettering. Phones get the site's `phone` tier and `-phone` floor textures (`public/assets/frequency-shift/laser-room/`, CC0, see `CREDITS.md`).
- Card art (`FrequencyShiftCardArt.jsx`, `src/styles/fs-card.css`) is a window into the same room, built like the Instinct card from the scene with the site's own home sign. The project card shows the monogram over the wordmark; the folder sheet shows the monogram alone, high. The stills are the bare room (`-base`: sign, lasers, smoke and fog off) and what the sign adds (`-sign`). Everything that moves is a looping video of what it adds to the room (`-show.mp4`): the beams, the smoke the sign and the beams light, and the fog bank rolling along the floor. All of it is added back with plus-lighter. The card's show is the fan then the twin sheets, four bars each (32 beats), over a fog bank at 0.7 (the scene's `fog` option); about 1.6MB at 960x600. The sheet's show is the ceiling's rain, columns of light falling straight down into the full fog bank (about 260KB at 544x368). The site only brings the rain in as the camera turns to the floor, so the bake scrolls the page a screen with the camera's tilt at 0 (`turn=1`); as on the site, that thins the show's own beams to one in four. The sheet's tunnel (it spun) and the site's floor sheet (a flat slab at that size) were tried and dropped. Each loop is one pass rendered frame by frame at 30fps, with its last 2 seconds faded over its first, since the smoke never repeats. It loads only once the card nears the screen, plays only while it's there, and shows its first frame as a still before then and with reduced motion. Pointed at, the sign stutters through a power-on and the room tilts with the pointer. Re-bake after changing the scene: with `npm run dev` running, run `npm run bake:fs` and open each URL it prints in the browser pane (`scripts/fs-bake.html`). Without `?post` it shows every look side by side, or the whole room at `?times=`. `ONLY=video`, `ONLY=plates` or `ONLY=sheet` bakes a part. The receiver needs `cwebp` and `ffmpeg`, and the sign artwork for the bake lives in `scripts/fs-bake/`, so it doesn't ship.
- The web section's browser frame shows the live homepage (`web/home-laser-room.webp`). It was captured from frequencyshift.ca in the browser pane at 1280x720: the room's loop was stopped so one frame held, the page scaled 2x, and four tiles stitched (the header's blur was patched from a tile centred on the seam). There's no headless Chrome.
- `?laser-debug` shows the room's pace. `?laser-debug&laser-t=20` holds the show at 20s, and puts the handle on `window.fsLaserRoom`, so frames can be judged in a hidden or throttled browser pane (call `fsLaserRoom.freeze(t)` again after a scroll).

## Frequency Shift film reel

`FilmReel` draws the four films on one curved row in WebGL (`src/scenes/reel/`: `geometry.js` is the pure maths and hit test, `scene.js` the three.js side). Each video decodes once, and three.js and the videos load only as the row nears the screen. The markup is a row of poster buttons, shown as a still row for reduced motion, Save-Data, low-memory phones or no WebGL2. When the live row runs, they stay as its invisible keyboard route, and focusing one brings its film to the centre.

The row uses silent H.264 copies in `public/assets/frequency-shift/video/reel/`, the same length as the masters so the expanded player can carry on from the same moment. A changed master needs its copy re-encoded: 960x540 or 540x960, 30fps, no audio, CRF 27, `-maxrate 1600k -g 60 -movflags +faststart`.
